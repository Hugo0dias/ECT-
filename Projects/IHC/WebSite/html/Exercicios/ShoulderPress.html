Shoulder Press

Primary Muscles: Deltoids, Triceps, Upper Chest

✔️ In your favourites

Instructions

  1. Sit on a bench with back support or stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing forward
  3. Keep core engaged and maintain a slight arch in your lower back
  4. Press the weights upward until arms are fully extended overhead
  5. Slowly lower back to starting position with control
  6. Avoid locking elbows at the top and keep movement controlled
