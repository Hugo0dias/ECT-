Lying Dumbbell Extension

Primary Muscles: Triceps, Chest (secondary)

✔️ In your favourites

Instructions

  1. Lie flat on a bench with feet firmly planted on the floor
  2. Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other, arms extended straight up over chest
  3. Keep upper arms stationary as you slowly bend elbows to lower weights toward forehead
  4. Lower until dumbbells are near ears (90° elbow bend)
  5. Extend arms back to starting position using triceps strength
  6. Maintain control throughout, avoiding excessive arching in lower back

Tips

  • Use lighter weights than your bench press capacity
  • Keep elbows tucked in slightly (not flared outward)
  • Move slowly during eccentric (lowering) phase
