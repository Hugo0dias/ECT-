Squat

Primary Muscles: Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings

✔️ In your favourites

Instructions

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out
  2. Keep chest up and core engaged as you push hips back and bend knees
  3. Lower until thighs are parallel to the ground (or as low as comfortable)
  4. Drive through heels to return to standing position
  5. Keep knees aligned with toes throughout movement
